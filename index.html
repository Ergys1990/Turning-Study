Turning & Circulation Studio

Created, scripted and maintained by

Skidmore, Owings & Merrill · Washington DC

A swept-path and site-circulation tool for testing vehicle access on real plans: design vehicles drive your routes within their true steering limits, then the results become colour-coded circulation exhibits, animations and CAD files.

In this version

  • Vehicle kinematics: rate-limited steering, steering at standstill, trailer-guided reversing with jackknife limits, Ackermann front wheels.
  • Manufacturer-style vehicle profiles and a draggable Design Vehicles legend that docks into the side column.
  • Step-by-step tutorial, Export & Save menu, collapsible controls (☰).

References — AASHTO Green Book design vehicles · NFPA 1901 · IFC Appendix D · Manual for Streets · Approved Document B Vol. B5 · BS 5906. Results are kinematic studies for design; confirm fire-access requirements with the AHJ and verify with a licensed engineer.
